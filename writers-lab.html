How Writers Lab works

Practice technical-writing skills with focused prompts, realistic scenarios, and clear scoring rules.

  1. 01

    Choose your path

    Select your experience and level, then choose a skill block.

  2. 02

    Submit or skip

    Submit to see feedback; the next prompt opens automatically after a short pause. Use Previous to revisit, or Skip to move on for 0 points.

  3. 03

    Use hints carefully

    A hint gives guidance, but an accepted response earns half the available points.

  4. 04

    Review the block

    Submitted writing unlocks one possible answer at block review. Skipped and unanswered prompts stay locked until submitted; quizzes show the correct answer and explanation.

Each writing prompt has a Constraints box with its audience, format, style guidance, limits, and checks. Use Save progress to keep your work; unsaved work is cleared when you leave or reload. Block cards show counts from your latest save.

See the writing verdict rules
Accepted
Passes the style checks and scores above 85% on the semantic rubric.
Style Violation (SV)
Misses a deterministic style-guide rule.
Technical Inaccuracy (TI)
Misses a critical fact or scores 85% or below semantically.
Scope/Limit Exceeded (SLE)
Exceeds a word or sentence-length limit.
Structure Error (SE)
Misses required sections or formatting.
